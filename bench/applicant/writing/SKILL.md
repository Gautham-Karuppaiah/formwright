# Writing job-specific answers for the bench applicant

## The shape

Three short paragraphs: what the company does that he wants to work on, the one piece of his work closest to it,
and what he would do in the first months. Under 200 words.

## Hard rules

- Only facts from the resume and the profile.
- No adjectives about himself ("passionate", "hard-working").
- Never point out a gap to excuse it.
